About the Event

Build with AI.
Innovate with Purpose.
Pitch for Impact.

Summer School 2026 — AI First Hackathon at IIT Jammu in collaboration with Techible & I3C is a national-level innovation challenge bringing together students from across India to build impactful, real-world AI solutions.

Over three competitive rounds, participants will move from idea → functional prototype, leveraging cutting-edge technologies across the full spectrum of modern AI.

Generative AI LLMs AI Agents Computer Vision ML Multimodal AI Automation
🚀

Rapid Prototyping

From concept to working MVP in a time-boxed sprint environment designed for innovation.

🤝

Interdisciplinary Collaboration

Teams of 2–3 from diverse backgrounds building across domains.

💡

Entrepreneurial Thinking

Innovation over perfection — originality, feasibility, and real impact matter most.

🎯

Expert Jury

Live demo sessions before industry-leading AI professionals and researchers.
